Survivors

Evans City, PA

The dead are walking. You and the others are holed up in the old farmhouse off Ash Stop Road. The grid is failing, but GPS still works, and you have a drone.

Controls

Keys: W/S forward/back, A/D sideways, Q/E or ←/→ turn, R/F or ↑/↓ up/down, T/G camera tilt, M map, C view, H return home.

Touch: left pad climbs and turns, right pad moves.

Map: tap a spot, pick an altitude, and the drone flies there. Any stick input takes back control.
