Survivors

Evans City, PA

The dead are walking. You and the others are holed up in the old farmhouse off Ash Stop Road. The grid is failing, but GPS still works, and you have a drone.

Controls

Keys: W/S forward/back, A/D sideways, Q/E or ←/→ turn, R/F or ↑/↓ up/down, T/G camera tilt, P photo, M map, C view, H return home, B the house.

Touch: left pad climbs and turns, right pad moves.

Map: tap a spot, pick an altitude, and the drone flies there. Any stick input takes back control.

Playing: photograph places with the drone. Whoever is watching the feed at the house will tell you what they see. Back at the house, send people on supply runs to the places you photographed, or to talk to anyone you found alive, then end the day.
